My parents especially my mother have seen the clearest way of how human psychology works when you have money and when you haven't.

Scenraio 1 : when my fateher was unemployed and had no money, he used to do politics for sarpanch and his ideal routine was wakeup have breakfast, go with friends throughout the day do politics stuff and come drunk at night have fight with mother because of the what people and family member says, then sleep.

At this time all the relatives used to hate, my parents and always give taunts and literally no kind people always saying bad words front and back and literally no respect even his small brother, and other relatives. Even after helping too many people throughout the block in terms of everything without food and water, still at the end they betrayed for few money from opponents.

Then thankfully one day, my grand nani came and literally thrashed my father with words and motivated him to go for work so he left the village and politics behind.
