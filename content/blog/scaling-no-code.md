# The Rise of No-Code

No-code tools have revolutionized how we build software. But can they scale?

## The Benefits

1. **Speed to Market**: Build prototypes in days, not months.
2. **Lower Cost**: Reduces the need for expensive engineering teams.
3. **Empowerment**: Allows non-technical founders to launch.

## The Challenges

However, there are limits. Performance at scale and vendor lock-in are real
concerns.
