I am 21 years old, working in an MNC, without a degree.

If someone had told me this a few years ago, I probably would not have believed them.

For a long time, I assumed people working in MNCs came from a very different world. In my mind, they had everything sorted:

- degrees from the right colleges
- proper plans
- polished communication
- clear direction

And then there was me.

I was just a guy spending long hours on the internet trying to learn things on my own.

While many people around me were focused on exams and placements, I was spending most of my time in online communities. I loved interacting with people, helping where I could, answering doubts, sharing resources, and being around ambitious builders online.

I was not an expert.
Most of the time, I was learning along with everyone else.

But those communities changed the way I thought about life.

I got exposed to people who were:

- building real products
- learning practical skills quickly
- working remotely
- creating opportunities for themselves
- choosing paths outside the traditional script

That changed my confidence slowly and permanently.

I stopped seeing career as a fixed ladder and started seeing it as a compounding process. Skill by skill. Project by project. Conversation by conversation.

Today, I work at Enviu, and it has already been 8 months.

Sometimes it still feels unreal.

I am often the youngest person in the team, and every day I get to learn from talented people around me. That alone feels like a privilege.

I still do not have everything figured out.
I am still learning.
I am still experimenting.
I am still becoming.

And maybe that is the point.

You do not need to have the perfect map before starting.
You need curiosity, consistency, and the courage to keep showing up.
