Most people say games are addictive because they help us escape reality.

For me, that was never the main hook.

When I was a kid, I used to play GTA San Andreas for hours. Sometimes I would sit for five to six hours just to complete a mission.

What kept me there was not just the game world. It was the loop.

Each mission stretched me a little beyond my current ability:

- not easy enough to get bored
- not hard enough to quit
- clear objective
- immediate feedback
- visible progress

That edge made it sticky.

Now I see the same pattern in vibe coding, but stronger.

The loop is still there, but now most constraints are removed.

I define the objective.
The difficulty scales with how far I want to push.
Feedback is instant and far more meaningful.
The reward is not just completion, it is something useful that can exist outside my screen.

## Why games flatten over time

Most games eventually collapse once you understand their mechanics.

You learn the system.
You optimize your actions.
Challenge drops.
Excitement fades.

That curve is normal because games are bounded systems.

## Why coding does not flatten the same way

With vibe coding, I am building on top of a general-purpose machine.

There is no fixed path.
There is no final level.
There is no hard ceiling.

Every solved problem opens a new problem.
Every project creates a new possibility.

The loop keeps renewing itself because the problem space is open-ended.

## Real proof from a midnight build

A few weeks ago, I built something at midnight just for my own use case.

I shared it on Reddit and overnight it got around 1000 users.
Now it sees roughly 100 daily active users with average usage time around 20 minutes each.

Project link: [Midnight build](https://lnkd.in/dPvMg765)

That moment made the difference very clear to me.

In gaming, effort creates in-game progress.
In building, effort creates real-world utility.

## Final reflection

I still enjoy games, but I no longer chase them for the same dopamine loop.

I have found a better loop:

- challenge with freedom
- instant feedback with real consequences
- progress that compounds into tangible outcomes

For me, vibe coding feels like the evolved version of what gaming once gave me.
