Why I didn't continued Iot Cattle health monitoring I paused the IoT cattle health monitoring project because it clashed with my core principles. Animals are living beings deserving real care, not machines optimized just for milk yield. Village farmers already watch their cows closely during daily feeding and checkups, showing attentive husbandry works without turning them into data points.

Nature is brutal with predators eating prey and us consuming plants that are also alive and often pumped with chemicals for better yield. Veganism is not feasible for everyone's budget or nutrition needs, especially my gym macros. Still, humans can aim higher than survival cycles.

Mechanizing dairy risks more exploitation than compassion, even if IoT detects illnesses early. For now, traditional respect feels right, so I will innovate elsewhere that aligns better.
