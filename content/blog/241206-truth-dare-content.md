--x------ fun dares to challenge people:

1. what's the most uncomfortable thing that can be done in this situation. DO IT.

2. Send a random compliment to the last person you texted and screenshot their reaction.

3. Post an embarrassing photo of yourself on your social media story for 24 hours.

4. Call your crush or ex and confess one thing you wish you had told them.

5. Eat or drink something weird (e.g., a mix of condiments or an odd food pairing).

6. Share your most recent search history from your phone without editing it.

7. Let someone in the group send a random message from your phone to a contact of their choice.

8. Do 10 push-ups and yell out the name of your biggest fear after each one.

9. Dance to a cringy song of the group’s choice and record a 15-second video to share.

10. Swap phones with someone for 5 minutes and let them explore your apps (no deletions!).
