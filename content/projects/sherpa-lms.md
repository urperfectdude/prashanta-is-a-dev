## Overview

Sherpa LMS redefines corporate training by making it engaging and social. Moved
away from traditional boring video lectures to interactive, gamified learning
paths that reward progress and collaboration.

## Key Features

- **Gamified Progress**: Points, badges, and leaderboards for completing
  modules.
- **Peer Learning**: Discussion forums and group challenges.
- **Interactive Content**: Quizzes, flashcards, and drag-and-drop exercises.
- **No-Code Build**: Rapidly developed and iterated using Bubble.io.

## Tech Stack

- **Platform**: Bubble.io
- **Design**: Figma
- **Integrations**: Zapier, SendGrid

## Impact

- Increased course completion rates from 30% to **85%**.
- Received 4.8/5 user satisfaction rating from pilot group.
