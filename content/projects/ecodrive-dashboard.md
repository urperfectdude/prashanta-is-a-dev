## Overview

EcoDrive Dashboard is a comprehensive solution for managing electric vehicle
fleets. It provides real-time insights into vehicle performance, battery health,
and driver behavior to optimize operations and reduce costs.

## Key Features

- **Real-time Telemetry**: Live tracking of speed, battery level, and location.
- **Predictive Maintenance**: AI-driven alerts for potential battery issues.
- **Charging Optimization**: Smart scheduling to utilize off-peak energy rates.
- **Driver Scorecards**: Gamified metrics to encourage eco-friendly driving.

## Tech Stack

- **Mobile App**: Flutter
- **Backend**: Supabase (PostgreSQL + Realtime)
- **IoT Integration**: MQTT, Python
- **Analytics**: Mixpanel

## Impact

- Improved fleet battery life by **15%**.
- Reduced operational costs by **20%** through optimized charging.
