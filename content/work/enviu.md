## Overview

At Enviu, I am responsible for leading the development of scalable mobile and
web applications. My role involves end-to-end product building, from
architecture to deployment, ensuring high performance and user satisfaction.

## Key Contributions

- **Deadstock Marketplace**: Took a deadstock marketplace from kickoff to
  **₹374K GMV in 45 days**, moving 400+ vendor items that had been sitting in
  warehouses with no channel to sell them.
- **Scoping**: Ran 15+ vendor interviews to map the deadstock process end to
  end, then turned what warehouse ops, vendor success and engineering each
  wanted into one prioritized roadmap. It shipped as a single connected system
  where vendors, admins and warehouse staff each see their own screens.
- **Checkout**: Tracked the funnel from browse to checkout, redesigned the
  pricing step around where buyers actually dropped off, and added WhatsApp and
  email nudges for carts left mid-checkout.
- **Vendor Listing**: Manual photos and write-ups were holding back how fast
  vendors could list stock. Shipped AI cataloging that cut listing time by
  **60%**.
- **Stack**: LangGraph, Flutter, Supabase, Postgres, Bubble.io, Cloudflare,
  Mixpanel, Crashlytics.
